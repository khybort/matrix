Pre-registration — signal research 2026-10, round 4: dated-futures basis carry ("H13")
Written 2026-10-09 16:53 UTC (date -u), before any return was computed and before any price, delivery price, lending rate
series or order book for this round was fetched. Only endpoint availability was probed (listed under DATA).
Earlier rounds: ../signal_2026_10/PREREG.txt, ../signal_2026_10_r2/, ../signal_2026_10_r3/, ../signal_2026_10_r3b/.

QUESTION. Every funding carry except H1 failed because the yield is a floating rate (funding) that reverses in squeezes.
Long spot + short a DATED future held to expiry locks its yield at entry: the future converges to the index at delivery.
Return = entry basis - fees - spread - settlement tracking error, on capital = spot + futures margin. Does it beat a
risk-free stablecoin rate out of sample, after costs, at a size worth trading?

STRUCTURES (both are "long spot + short dated future, equal USD exposure")
 LIN  Binance USD-M (USDT-margined) quarterly. Spot bought with USDT on Binance spot; the short future on isolated margin
      N/L, L in {1, 2, 3}. Capital = N + N/L. Liquidation possible (rule below).
 INV  coin-margined (Binance COIN-M, Deribit). The spot coin bought (Binance spot, USDT) IS the collateral; short contracts
      worth C = N * F0/S0 USD, which locks the USD value at N*F0/S0 (fully hedged incl. the basis profit). Capital = N.
      Effective leverage stays 1x: account equity in coin = C/P_t, cannot be liquidated (reported, checked on data).
      USDT ~ USD assumed (Deribit/COIN-M settle on USD indices); the USDT/USD drift is not modelled.

TRANCHE RULE (per cell; one decision per group per day)
 Decision each day at 08:00 UTC on the 1h bars ending 08:00. Candidates: the group's live quarterly / bi-quarterly
 contracts (Binance: current and next quarter; Deribit: every quarterly) with 14 <= days-to-expiry <= 200.
 For each candidate: net annualised basis at decision
     b_net = ((F/S - 1) - cost_rt) * 365 / DTE,  cost_rt = 4 taker fees + the four walks at the primary size (below).
 If the best candidate has b_net >= Y, open ONE tranche of notional N in it, executed at the close of the 1h bars ending
 09:00 (one full bar after the decision). ALT: the rule runs per alt coin (one tranche per coin per day).
 Y in {5, 8, 12} %/yr.
 Exit:
   HOLD   to delivery. Future settles at the venue's published delivery price D; spot is sold as a TWAP over the
          venue's averaging window before delivery (Binance 60 min, Deribit 30 min), proxied by the mean of Binance spot 1m
          closes in that window. Tracking error = TWAP_spot - D (enters P&L).
   EARLY  checked daily at 08:00 UTC: exit at the 09:00 closes when the remaining gross annualised basis
          ((F/S - 1) * 365 / DTE) <= Z = 2 %/yr and DTE > 1; else HOLD. Re-entry follows the entry rule (new tranche).
 => cells: LIN {BTC, ETH} x Y(3) x exit(2) x L {1, 2, 3} = 36; INV {Binance BTC, Binance ETH, Binance ALT, Deribit BTC,
    Deribit ETH} x Y(3) x exit(2) = 30. m for this round = 66.

P&L per tranche (USD on spot notional N)
 spot leg   N * (S_exit / S0 - 1)   [S_exit = settlement TWAP or the 09:00 close on EARLY]
 future leg LIN: N * (F0 - F_exit)/F0 with F_exit = D or the 09:00 close. INV: C*(1/F_exit - 1/F0) coin, valued at S_exit.
 fees       taker per leg on traded notional: Binance spot 10.0 bps, Binance USD-M / COIN-M futures 5.0 bps, Deribit 5.0 bps;
            delivery charged as a taker leg (5.0 bps; conservative). 4 legs per tranche.
 spread     each of the four fills walked against mid on TODAY's full books (same method as rounds 1-3b) at the tranche size;
            on HOLD the closing future fill is the delivery (no walk) and the spot TWAP sale is walked at size/30 per slice
            (approximated by the walk at size/30).
 no funding, no borrow. Capital cost enters only through the benchmark below.
 Sizes: $500, $5k, $50k per leg. PRIMARY (decision) size: $5k. Others reported.
 Liquidation (LIN, L in {1,2,3}, no top-up): liquidated at the first hour whose MARK-price high >= F0 * (1 + 1/L - 0.01)
   (maintenance margin 1 %). Then the future margin N/L is lost entirely, spot is sold at that hour's close (taker + walk),
   the tranche ends.
 Return on capital r = PnL / capital; annualised a = r * 365 / hold_days; EXCESS e = a - rf (rf averaged over the hold).

STATISTICS
 Per cell: n tranches, mean e, median, money-weighted APR (sum PnL / sum capital*days * 365) minus capital-weighted rf,
 t clustered by ENTRY ISO WEEK (primary; Student t, G-1 df, one-sided p).
 SPLIT BY ENTRY TIME: TRAIN 2021-01-01 .. 2024-06-30, HOLDOUT 2024-07-01 .. 2026-10-09. Only tranches whose exit (delivery
 or early) happens by 2026-10-09 are evaluated; HOLD tranches in contracts delivering later are excluded and counted.
 Train pass: mean e > 0 and week-clustered t >= 2. Holdout evaluated once, only for train passers, rule frozen.
 Holdout success requires ALL of:
   (1) mean e > 0 and week-clustered t >= 2;
   (2) Benjamini-Yekutieli q <= 0.05 over the programme, m = 38 + 29 + 18 + 6 + 66 = 157 (H1's holdout p as reported,
       every other prior hypothesis p = 1);
   (3) for LIN cells: liquidation is included in (1) (no separate rule).
 Reported (not decision rules): t clustered by contract (expiry) — tranches on one contract share their settlement and
 basis regime; mean e at $500 and $50k; e vs flat 5 %; holdout by quarter; share of days the rule is in market;
 entry basis distribution; settlement tracking error distribution.

RISK (descriptive, every tranche)
 - max adverse mark-to-market of the short future within the hold, % of N (mark-price high vs F0);
 - LIN: share liquidated at 1x / 2x / 3x without top-ups; top-up a real account would need to never be liquidated
   = max over hours of (N * (M_t/F0 - 1) + 0.01 * N * M_t/F0 - N/L)^+, p50 / p90 / max;
 - INV: verify on data that coin equity never reaches maintenance (expected: never).
 - qualitative venue / counterparty notes (custody on the exchange for the whole hold; Deribit/Binance specific).
