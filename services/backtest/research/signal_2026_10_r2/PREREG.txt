Pre-registration — signal research 2026-10, round 2 (tick data)
Written 2026-10-09 13:40 UTC, before any return was computed. Round 1: ../signal_2026_10/PREREG.txt.

GOAL. Signals whose GROSS edge per episode is large enough (target >= 45 bps, 3x a taker round trip)
to survive realistic costs. Smaller effects are recorded but cannot be implemented.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DATA
 T1 tick panel. Bybit public trade archive public.bybit.com/trading/<SYM>/<SYM><DATE>.csv.gz
    (aggressor side per print; RPI prints dropped). 40 crypto USDT linear perps (symbolType '',
    launched before 2026-03-01), chosen as the top 40 by Bybit turnover over 2026-04-01..04-30 from
    round 1's 1h klines (no information after the train start). Days 2026-04-24 .. 2026-10-08
    (the first 7 days are warm-up only). Each symbol-day is reduced to 1-minute bars and the raw file
    is deleted: open/high/low/close, buy/sell aggressor USD, print counts, aggressor USD in prints
    >= $10k / $50k / $250k / $1M per side, and a spread proxy (below).
 T2 Binance USD-M 1m klines (data.binance.vision) for the same 40 symbols and days (H10 only).
 K1 round 1's 1h klines, 1h premium index, funding settlements (791 perps, 2025-10-01..2026-10-09)
    and 1h OI (top 200), plus Bybit 5m klines fetched for each H9 settlement window.
 Survivorship: every source lists perps trading on 2026-10-09 only. Delisted coins are absent.

SPLIT BY TIME (holdout evaluated once, only for train passers, rule frozen)
 T1/T2 hypotheses: TRAIN 2026-05-01..2026-07-31, HOLDOUT 2026-08-01..2026-10-08 (by entry time).
 K1 hypotheses:    TRAIN 2025-10-01..2026-05-31, HOLDOUT 2026-06-01..2026-10-08.

EXECUTION AND COSTS
 Decision at the close of the signal bar t (all inputs <= t). Tick hypotheses enter at the close of
 minute t+1 (one full minute skipped) and exit at the close of minute t+1+H. Kline hypotheses skip
 one full bar of their resolution (stated per hypothesis).
 Cost per round trip = 2 x 5.5 bps taker + half the spread at entry + half the spread at exit.
  - Tick spread proxy: at every print, bid = last sell-aggressor price, ask = last buy-aggressor
    price, both <= 10 s old and ask > bid; spread = (ask-bid)/mid. Per minute: median. A minute
    without a value uses that symbol-day's median. (Prints walk the book, so this overstates the
    touch spread: conservative.)
  - Kline hypotheses: spread from a map log(spread) = a + b log(daily turnover) fitted on every
    tick symbol-day available (panel + 424 September symbol-days of the maker study), floored at
    2 bps, applied with the coin's trailing-7d mean daily turnover.
 Funding: every settlement crossed while a position is open is charged/credited at its actual rate.
 One signal per episode: per (cell, symbol) no new entry before the previous exit.

STATISTICS
 Per cell: mean net bps per episode, n, number of days; t from a day-clustered standard error
 (cluster = UTC entry date). Week-clustered t also reported for holds >= 24 h.
 TRAIN PASS: net > 0 and day-clustered t >= 2.
 HOLDOUT SURVIVE: net > 0 and day-clustered t >= 2.
 IMPLEMENT only if it survives AND holdout one-sided p < 0.05 / k (k = cells sent to holdout) AND
 holdout net >= +15 bps per episode. Every cell below counts toward m; BY q-values over all m train
 p-values are reported.
 Thresholds below may be changed ONLY on the basis of trigger COUNTS in train (target 100..5000
 train episodes per cell), before any return of that cell is computed; every change is appended.

HYPOTHESES (m = 28 cells)

H8a-flow  aggressor imbalance (T1). Window W = last 15 min. B, S = buy/sell aggressor USD,
  I = (B-S)/(B+S), V = B+S. Vbar = mean of 15-min V over the trailing 7 days.
  Trigger |I| >= 0.35 and V >= 4 Vbar.
  Cells: follow sign(I) H=60m; follow H=240m; fade H=60m; fade H=240m.              (4)

H8a-large  large-print clusters (T1). Per symbol the "large" bucket is the smallest of
  {$10k,$50k,$250k,$1M} whose train mean is <= 300 large prints per day (counts only).
  L = net large aggressor USD over the last 15 min; sigma = std of L over the trailing 7 days
  (one value per minute, rolling). Trigger |L| >= 5 sigma.
  Cells: follow sign(L) H=60m; follow H=240m; fade H=60m; fade H=240m.              (4)

H8b  liquidation-cascade proxy, bounce (T1). Always FADE the move.
  (1m) minute return |r| >= max(1.0 %, 6 x std of 1m returns over trailing 24 h), same-side
       aggressor share >= 0.70 in the direction of the move, minute volume >= 8x trailing-24h mean.
  (5m) 5-min return |r| >= max(2.0 %, 5 x std of 5m returns over trailing 24 h), same-side share
       >= 0.65, 5-min volume >= 5x trailing-24h mean 5-min volume.
  Cells: {1m, 5m} x H in {30m, 60m, 240m}.                                          (6)
  Bybit/Binance do not publish historical liquidations (Binance liquidationSnapshot: 404).

H9  funding-settlement microstructure (K1 + 5m windows). Universe at settlement T: crypto perp
  (symbolType '' or 'innovation'), listed >= 30 days, trailing-7d mean daily turnover >= $2M.
  Predicted rate at T-30m: interval >= 2h: P = mean of the 1h premium-index closes of the bars
  of the current interval that closed by T-1h; F^ = P + clamp(0.01 % x interval/8h - P, -0.05 %,
  +0.05 %). Interval 1h: F^ = the rate settled at T-1h. Interval = gap from the previous settlement.
  PRE: enter at the close of the 5m bar ending T-30m, exit at the close of the bar ending T (last
       trade before settlement, no funding). Side against the payer: short if F^ > 0, long if F^ < 0.
       Cells |F^| >= 0.10 %, |F^| >= 0.30 %.                                         (2)
  POST: realised settled rate F (known at T). Enter at the close of the 5m bar ending T+5m
       (skip 5 min), hold 30m or 240m; side = reversal of the payer pressure: long if F > 0, short
       if F < 0. Cells |F| in {>= 0.10 %, >= 0.30 %} x {30m, 240m}.                   (4)

H10  cross-venue lead-lag (T2 vs T1). g = Binance 5-min return - Bybit 5-min return at minute t
  (both closes). Trigger |g| >= 30 bps. Trade Bybit in the direction of g.
  Cells H=15m, H=60m.                                                                (2)

H11  OI shock + price (K1 1h, OI top 200). At hour close T: dOI = log OI change over 4h,
  r = log price change over 4h. Universe as H9. Entry at the close of the bar ending T+1h.
  build-up: dOI >= +15 % and |r| >= 3 %  -> FOLLOW sign(r).
  flush:    dOI <= -15 % and |r| >= 3 %  -> FADE.
  Cells {build-up, flush} x H in {4h, 24h}.                                          (4)

H12  new-listing short (K1 + instruments launchTime). Every crypto USDT perp launched
  2025-10-01..2026-09-24. Decision at launch + 24 h, entry SHORT at the close of the 1h bar ending
  launch + 25 h, hold 7 d or 14 d, funding charged. Split by entry date. Spread from the map with
  the coin's mean daily turnover over its first 24 h. Delisted listings are absent (they mostly
  fell, so this understates a short).
  Cells H=7d, H=14d.                                                                 (2)
