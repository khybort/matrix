Pre-registration — signal research 2026-10, round 3: positive-funding mirror of H1
Written 2026-10-09 15:25 UTC (date -u), before any return was computed and before any spot kline or
order book for this round was fetched. Round 1: ../signal_2026_10/PREREG.txt. Round 2: ../signal_2026_10_r2/PREREG.txt.

QUESTION. H1 (long perp + short spot after a settlement <= -0.08 %) lost most of its edge to borrow
realism. The mirror needs no loan: after an extreme POSITIVE settlement, short the perp and buy spot,
collect funding. Does it pay after realistic costs, out of sample?

------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
DATA
 Round 1's year of Bybit public history (791 USDT linear perps trading on 2026-10-09): funding
 settlements with actual intervals, 1h klines (OHLC, turnover), 1h premium index.
 Spot hedge: 1h spot klines per coin, Bybit spot <COIN>USDT, else Binance spot <COIN>USDT (public
 endpoints), whole year, fetched after this file is committed. Coin = perp base with any
 1000/10000/100000/1000000 prefix removed (price ratios make the multiplier irrelevant).
 Order books: full public depth today (Bybit linear perp; spot book on the hedge venue), one fetch per
 coin, applied to every past episode of that coin (same method as the adversarial check / shb).
 Survivorship: delisted perps absent (as rounds 1-2).

SPLIT BY TIME: TRAIN 2025-10-01 .. 2026-05-31, HOLDOUT 2026-06-01 .. 2026-10-09 (by settlement time).
 Holdout evaluated once, only for cells that pass train, rule frozen. Holdouts of train failures may be
 computed afterwards for the record only.

EPISODE RULE (per cell, per symbol, no new entry before the previous exit)
 Signal: a settlement S with settled rate >= X (raw rate per settlement, as settled, as H1 used it and
 as cash_and_carry reads it). X in {0.05 %, 0.08 %, 0.15 %}.
 Entry E = S + 1h: short perp at the close of the 1h perp bar ending at E, long spot at the close of the
 1h spot bar ending at E, equal USD notional. Needs both prices; no spot price at E or at exit = no
 episode (point-in-time spot listing; the hedge venue is Bybit if it has a price at E, else Binance).
 Exit:
  - fixed hold h in {24, 48, 96} h: exit at E + h;
  - flip variant of each hold: exit at the first settlement t in (E, E+h] with rate <= 0, at the close
    of the bar ending at ceil_hour(t) + 1h (one-hour lag as at entry), else at E + h.
 => 3 thresholds x 3 holds x {fixed, flip} = 18 cells.

P&L per episode (bps of one leg's notional)
 funding  = + sum of settled rates over settlements in (E, exit], each times perp price at that
            settlement / perp entry price (mark-to-market notional); actual interval, every raw
            settlement counted once.
 hedge    = (spot exit/spot entry - 1) - (perp exit/perp entry - 1)   [long spot, short perp]
 cost     = 4 taker fees: perp 5.5 bps x2 + spot 10 bps x2 (Bybit spot VIP0 0.1 %, Binance spot VIP0
            0.1 %) = 31 bps, plus the four walked fills against mid on today's books at $500 per leg
            (perp sell + buy, spot buy + sell). No book today for either leg = episode excluded and
            counted.
 borrow   = none (spot is bought, not borrowed). No financing charge on the perp margin or spot cash.
 net      = funding + hedge - cost.

STATISTICS
 Per cell: n, mean net, median, 5 %-trimmed mean, t clustered by ISO week (primary) and by day.
 Train pass: mean net > 0 and week-clustered t >= 2.
 Holdout success: mean net > 0, week-clustered t >= 2, AND Benjamini-Yekutieli q <= 0.05 over the whole
 programme: m = 38 (round 1) + 29 (round 2) + 18 (this round) = 85. One-sided holdout p from the
 week-clustered t (Student t, G-1 df). Prior rounds' p-values: H1 holdout p as reported, every other
 prior hypothesis p = 1 (conservative: they cannot raise the step-up rank).
 Reported for every cell (not decision rules): excluding the top 5 % of episodes, the $5k-per-leg cost,
 fees x2, top-5-coin / top-10-day share of PnL, holdout by month.

BASIS / SQUEEZE RISK (descriptive, every train/holdout episode of each cell)
 - entry basis = perp/spot - 1 at E; premium index at S;
 - within the hold, hourly: worst hedge mark (min over hours of hedge P&L), worst position mark
   (hedge + funding accrued to that hour), max perp run-up (max 1h high / entry - 1) as a liquidation
   proxy for the short perp leg when the spot leg sits on another venue or another margin account;
 - share of episodes whose perp run-up exceeds 20 % / 50 % (liquidation at 5x / 2x perp leverage
   without top-up).

IMPLEMENTATION RULE. If a cell survives, no new module: the result is mapped onto parameter changes of
the existing cash_and_carry (threshold, universe via the carry watchlist, hold, flip exit). If nothing
survives, the negatives are written up so the question stays closed.

Reference rows (not counted in m, no decision): cash_and_carry as it actually runs (strategy_configs
row v1: min_funding 0.0001, horizon 8h, 25-coin active universe proxy = round 1 active_universe.txt),
and the 18 cells restricted to that universe.
