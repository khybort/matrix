QUESTION. Options prices carry the market's fear/greed and insurance premium. Do extremes of the volatility risk premium,
25-delta skew, implied-vol spikes, IV term-structure inversion or put/call demand predict the BTC / ETH perp return over
3-7 days by enough to pay a taker round trip plus funding? Slow signals only: the target is >= 45 bps GROSS per episode.

DAILY FEATURES (asset in {BTC, ETH}; decision time D = 00:00 UTC each day; only data with timestamp < D)
 DVOL_D   DVOL close of the last 1h bar ending at or before D.
 RV30     annualised realised vol of the perp: std of 1h log returns over the 720 h before D * sqrt(8760), in vol points.
 VRP      DVOL_D - RV30.
 DVOLchg  ln(DVOL_D / DVOL_{D-24h}).
 From the option trades in [D-4h, D) (Black-76 delta with F = index_price, r = 0, T = time to the 08:00 UTC expiry,
 sigma = iv/100; strike, expiry and type parsed from instrument_name):
 RR25     median iv of calls with delta in [0.15, 0.35] minus median iv of puts with delta in [-0.35, -0.15], both with
          20 <= DTE <= 45. Missing if either side has < 5 trades.
 TERM     median iv of trades with |delta| in [0.40, 0.60] and 1.5 <= DTE <= 10 minus the same with 45 <= DTE <= 120.
          Missing if either bucket has < 5 trades.
 PC3      sum over the last three daily windows of put notional (amount * index_price) / the same for calls.
          Missing if any of the three windows has < 20 trades.
 Percentile pct(X)_D = share of the asset's non-missing daily values of X in the 365 calendar days before D that are
 < X_D. Requires >= 120 such values, else no signal that day.

CELLS (14; BTC and ETH episodes pooled in each cell; direction fixed here from the economic prior)
 A  volatility risk premium (Bollerslev-Tauchen-Zhou: high premium = priced fear, followed by higher returns)
    A.hi.3 / A.hi.7   pct(VRP) >= 0.90  -> LONG,  hold 72 h / 168 h
    A.lo.3 / A.lo.7   pct(VRP) <= 0.10  -> SHORT, hold 72 h / 168 h   (realised above implied = complacent pricing)
 B  25-delta skew as fear / greed reversal
    B.put.3 / B.put.7   pct(RR25) <= 0.10 (puts rich)  -> LONG
    B.call.3 / B.call.7 pct(RR25) >= 0.90 (calls rich) -> SHORT
 C  DVOL spike, fade the move that caused it
    C.3 / C.7   pct(DVOLchg) >= 0.95 -> trade AGAINST the sign of the perp return over [D-24h, D) (long if < 0, short if > 0)
 D  IV term-structure inversion (front > back = acute stress, contrarian)
    D.3 / D.7   pct(TERM) >= 0.90 -> LONG
 P  put/call demand extreme (contrarian)
    P.3 / P.7   pct(PC3) >= 0.90 -> LONG
 m for this round = 14.

EPISODES
 Signal window = a maximal run of consecutive decision days on which the cell's condition holds for that asset.
 One episode per signal window, entered on its first day; and only if no episode of the same (cell, asset) is open at D.
 A window that starts while an episode is open is skipped entirely (no re-entry later in the same window).
 Entry at the close of the Bybit 1h perp bar ending D + 1 h (one full bar after the decision). Exit at the close of the
 bar ending entry + hold. Equal notional per episode.

P&L per episode, bps of notional (Bybit linear perp)
 price      side * (P_exit / P_entry - 1) * 1e4
 funding    every Bybit settlement with entry < t_settle <= exit: - side * rate * (P_settle / P_entry) * 1e4
            (long pays positive funding; notional marked to market at the settlement hour's close).
 cost       2 * 5.5 bps taker + the measured round-trip spread: the two fills walked against mid on today's books at $5k
            per leg (median of the 5 snapshots, per asset).
 net = price + funding - cost.  gross = price + funding.

STATISTICS AND DECISION
 SPLIT BY ENTRY TIME: TRAIN 2021-03-24 .. 2024-06-30, HOLDOUT 2024-07-01 .. 2026-10-08 (episodes must exit by the last
 kline; later ones are dropped and counted). (The 365-day lookback means the first signals fall in ~2021-07.)
 Per cell: n, mean net, median, mean gross, t clustered by ENTRY ISO WEEK (Student t, G-1 df, one-sided p vs 0).
 Train pass: mean net > 0 and week-clustered t >= 2. Holdout evaluated once, only for train passers, rule frozen.
 Holdout success requires ALL of:
   (1) mean net > 0 and week-clustered t >= 2;
   (2) Benjamini-Yekutieli q <= 0.05 over the programme at the cumulative m read at decision time from the research
       ledger (docs/research/ledger.jsonl) if it exists, else 91 + 66 (round 4) + 14 = 171; H1's holdout p as reported,
       round 4's decision p's if available, every other prior hypothesis p = 1, this round's train failures p = 1.
 To be designed as a strategy module, a survivor also needs holdout mean net >= +15 bps.
 Reported (not decision rules): holdout of train failures (record only, after the decision); per-asset split; day count
 in market; trimmed (top/bottom 5 %) mean; cost x2; median; the signal's own frequency per year.
