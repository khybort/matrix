QUESTION. H1 (long Bybit perp + short spot after a Bybit settlement <= threshold, 48 h) survived round 1 but
its edge depends on spot borrow that is least available on squeezed coins. Replace the borrowed spot short
with a SHORT PERP ON ANOTHER VENUE (no borrow). P&L = Bybit funding received - hedge-venue funding paid
(or + received) + cross-venue perp basis change - costs. Does it pay out of sample?

EPISODE RULE (per cell, per Bybit symbol, no new entry before the previous exit)
 Signal: Bybit settlement S with raw settled rate <= X (rate per settlement, as H1 used it).
   X in {-0.08 %, -0.15 %}.
 Entry E = S + 1h. Long Bybit perp at the close of the 1h bar ending at E; short the hedge-venue perp at the
   close of its 1h bar ending at E; equal USD notional.
 Hedge venue: among eligible venues, the one whose last settled rate at or before E, normalised per 8 h
   (rate x 8 / that venue's current interval = gap between its last two settlements, clipped to [1, 8] h),
   is highest (least negative / most positive). Ties: Binance, OKX, Bitget, Gate. No eligible venue = no
   episode (counted).
 Exit (3 rules):
   F24  fixed: exit at E + 24 h.
   F48  fixed: exit at E + 48 h.
   CONV convergence: at each Bybit settlement t in (E, E + 96 h], compare Bybit's settled rate per 8 h with
        the hedge venue's last settled rate (<= t) per 8 h; at the first t where Bybit's is HIGHER, exit at
        the close of the bars ending at ceil_hour(t) + 1 h; else exit at E + 96 h.
 => 2 thresholds x 3 exits = 6 cells. m for this round = 6.

P&L per episode (bps of ONE leg's notional N)
 funding  = - sum of Bybit settled rates over Bybit settlements in (E, X]
            + sum of hedge-venue settled rates over hedge settlements in (E, X],
            each times that venue's price at the settlement (close of the 1h bar ending at ceil_hour(t)) /
            that venue's entry price (mark-to-market notional). Each venue's own timestamps and own
            interval; every raw settlement counted once; interval changes handled by summing raw rates.
 basis    = (Bybit exit/Bybit entry - 1) - (hedge exit/hedge entry - 1).
 cost     = 4 perp taker fees at VIP0: Bybit 5.5 bps x2 + hedge venue x2 (Binance 5.0, OKX 5.0,
            Bitget 6.0, Gate 5.0 bps), plus the four walked fills against mid on today's books at $500
            per leg (Bybit buy + sell, hedge sell + buy). Missing book on either side = excluded, counted.
 net      = funding + basis - cost. No borrow. No financing charge on margin.

CAPITAL (reported for every cell; decision on the first item below)
 - Unlevered: collateral N on each venue, capital 2N -> return on capital = net / 2.
 - Leverage L in {3, 5} per leg, isolated margin N/L on each venue, capital 2N/L ->
   return on capital = net_L x L / 2, where net_L is the liquidation-adjusted net:
   a leg is liquidated at the first hour in which its adverse move from entry (Bybit long: 1h low;
   hedge short: 1h high) reaches 1/L - 1 % (maintenance margin 1 %). Then: that leg loses its whole
   margin (-1/L of N), the other leg is closed at that hour's close (with its exit walk and fee), funding
   counts only settlements up to that hour, the episode ends. No top-ups.

 Train pass: mean net > 0 and week-clustered t >= 2.
 Holdout success requires ALL of:
   (1) mean net > 0 and week-clustered t >= 2;
   (2) Benjamini-Yekutieli q <= 0.05 over the whole programme: m = 38 (round 1) + 29 (round 2) + 18

       Prior rounds' p-values: H1 holdout p as reported, every other prior hypothesis p = 1;
   (3) mean liquidation-adjusted net at 3x leverage > 0 (the design must be deployable at 3x).
 Reported for every cell (not decision rules): excluding the top 5 % of episodes, cost at $5k per leg,
 fees x2, top-5-coin / top-10-day share of PnL, holdout by month, hedge-venue mix, funding vs basis split.
